What is PSTCRD

PSTCRD is an art project created to take back content from the algorithm.

PSTCRD is a student project created by Andrew Patra for ATLS 5204 - Product Development Studio taught by John Bacus at the ATLAS Institute at the Univeristy of Colorado Boulder.

How It Works

PSTCRD works best on mobile devices. The sender can take a 5 second clip from wherever they are in the world and send a video postcard to their recipent's email. Using a consent system, PSTCRD then asks the recipent to approve the sender and "open" the envelope. When approved, PSTCRD delivers your video postcard to your recipent's inbox.

Please note that while PSTCRD takes security seriously, this is also a studnet project. Please use caution when creating and sending.
