About PSTCRD

PSTCRD is a space for sharing small moments with people who matter.

PSTCRD (postcard) is an art project in the form of a peer to peer content service. PSTCRD takes curation, creation, and sharing out of the hands of the algorithms created by social media platforms and returns it to the creator. You create the content and you choose who to send it to, and most importantly, you must create to participate

How Does PSTCRD Work?

When you visit PSTCRD, you have the ability to create a 5 second video to send to your friends, family, or anyone who would like a video postcard in their email. After verifying your email as a sender, your recipient will recieve an "envelope" in the form of an email asking them to approve recieving your postcard. Once they approve it, they'll then recieve your 5 second recording along with the option to create one themselves.
