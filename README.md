# PSTCRD.ART

[PSTCRD.ART](https://www.pstcrd.art) (Postcard) is an art project created for ATLS 5204 - Product Development Studio taught by John Bacus at the ATLAS Institute at the Univeristy of Colorado Boulder. 

## What is it?

PSTCRD (postcard) is an art project in the form of a peer to peer content service. PSTCRD takes curation, creation, and sharing out of the hands of the algorithms created by social media platforms and returns it to the creator.

You create the content and you choose who to send it to, and most importantly, you must **create to participate**.
